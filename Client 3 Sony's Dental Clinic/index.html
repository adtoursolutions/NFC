About Us

Welcome to Sony's Dental Clinic Pala

Sony's Dental Clinic is a trusted dental care clinic in Pala, Kerala, dedicated to providing quality dental care in a comfortable and patient-friendly environment. Led by Dr. Sanju Mathew, Dental Surgeon, our clinic focuses on maintaining healthy teeth and gums while helping patients achieve confident, beautiful smiles.

Our Approach

We provide personalized dental care with a focus on your comfort, oral health, and a confident smile.

From routine dental care to root canal treatment, cosmetic dentistry, orthodontics, dental implants, and kids dentistry, we are here to meet your dental needs.

Our Services

General Dentistry

Comprehensive dental care focused on maintaining healthy teeth, gums, and overall oral health.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Dental treatments designed to enhance the appearance of your smile and help you feel more confident.

Dental Implants

Reliable tooth replacement solutions designed to restore function, comfort, and confidence.

Orthodontics

Dental care to help correct tooth alignment and improve the function and appearance of your smile.

Root Canal Treatment

Professional root canal care to treat infected or damaged teeth and help preserve your natural tooth.

Kids Dentistry

Friendly and comfortable dental care to support children's oral health and develop healthy dental habits.
