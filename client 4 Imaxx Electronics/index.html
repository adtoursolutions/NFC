About Us

Welcome to Imaxx Electronics Karur

Imaxx Electronics is Karur's dedicated destination for premium home entertainment, smart displays, and modern digital electronics. Located centrally at Kovai Main Road opposite EB station Renganayaki Puram, we bring world-class viewing experiences right to your home.

Founded and managed by Mohammed Ismail, Imaxx Electronics was built with a clear vision: to provide genuine branded televisions, honest advice, competitive prices, and reliable after-sales service to every household in Karur and surrounding regions.

Whether you are upgrading to a high-definition 4K smart TV, setting up an immersive home theatre, or seeking professional wall-mounting solutions, our team delivers personalized recommendations backed by authorized brand warranties.
