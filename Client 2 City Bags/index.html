About Us

Welcome to City Bags Karur

City Bags Karur is a trusted local destination for high-quality luggage, backpacks, handbags, and everyday bags. Established on September 9, 1998, we have been serving customers for over 25 years with a commitment to quality, value, and excellent customer service.

Our Story

Founded and led by Mohammed Luqman Ul Hakkim, City Bags was built with a simple vision — to provide durable, stylish, and affordable bags for every need. From everyday essentials to long journeys, we understand that the right bag plays an important role in our customers' daily lives.

Over the years, our dedication to quality products and customer satisfaction has helped us build a strong reputation among customers in Karur.
